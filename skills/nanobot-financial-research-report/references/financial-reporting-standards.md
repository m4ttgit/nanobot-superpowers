# Financial Reporting Standards#

Professional financial report structure and disclaimer templates for investment research.

## Standard Report Sections#

### 1. Executive Summary#
- Company name, fiscal year, currency#
- Key financial highlights (Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income, Total Assets)#
- Investment recommendation (Buy/Hold/Sell) with target price#
- Summary of key risks and opportunities#

### 2. Profitability Analysis#
- Revenue growth analysis (YoY/Boy)#
- Margin analysis (Gross, Operating, Net)#
- ROA and ROE trends#
- EBITDA performance vs. peers#
- **Flags:** Declining margins, negative ROE, contracting EBITDA#

### 3. Liquidity Analysis#
- Current Ratio and Quick Ratio trends#
- Working capital changes#
- Cash position and burn rate#
- **Flags:** Current Ratio <1.0x, Quick Ratio <1.0x, Negative Working Capital#

### 4. Leverage Analysis#
- Debt-to-Equity and Debt-to-Assets#
- Interest coverage ratio#
- Financial leverage trends#
- **Flags:** D/E >2.0x, Interest Coverage <2.5x, Over-leveraged#

### 5. Efficiency Analysis#
- Asset, Inventory, and Receivables Turnover#
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)#
- Cash Conversion Cycle#
- **Flags:** DS0 >60 days, DIO >90 days, Low turnover ratios#

### 6. Valuation Analysis (if market data)#
- P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA ratios#
- Comparison to industry peers#
- PEG ratio (growth-adjusted)#
- **Flags:** Overvalued (P/E >30), Undervalued (P/E <10)#

### 7. Risk Assessment#
- Red Flags (critical issues requiring immediate attention)#
- Yellow Flags (areas for improvement)#
- Mitigating factors#
- Scenario analysis (base/bull/bear cases)#

### 8. Investment Recommendation#
- Clear Buy/Hold/Sell with target price#
- Time horizon (short/medium/long-term)#
- Key catalysts and risk factors#
- Confidence level (High/Medium/Low)#

## Disclaimer Template#

```
DISCLAIMER: This report is for informational purposes only. 
Not financial advice. Consult a qualified financial advisor 
before making investment decisions.

Past performance does not guarantee future results.
All ratios and projections are based on provided data only.
```

## Common Pitfalls#

1. **Using one fiscal year** — Always show 3-5 years for trend analysis#
2. **Forgetting industry context** — Retail vs SaaS benchmarks differ significantly#
3. **Not adjusting for one-time items** — EBITDA should exclude restructuring charges, asset sales#
4. **Mixing calendar vs fiscal years** — Be consistent with 365 vs 360 days#
5. **Ignoring going concern** — Current Ratio <1.0x for 2+ quarters is a survival risk#

## Professional Formatting#

- Use **bold** for key metrics and ratios#
- Flag critical issues with 🔴 Red Flag, moderate with 🟡 Yellow Flag#
- Include peer comparison table (if valuation section)#
- Keep executive summary under 1 page#
- Append complete ratio tables in Appendix#
