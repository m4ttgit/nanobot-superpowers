# Audit Standards Reference

## Audit Opinion Types
1. **Unqualified (Clean)**: Financial statements present fairly in all material respects
2. **Qualified**: Except for a specific issue, financial statements are fair
3. **Adverse**: Financial statements do not present fairly — material misstatement
4. **Disclaimer**: Auditor unable to form an opinion (scope limitation)

## Key PCAOB Standards
- **AS 3101**: The Auditor's Report on an Audit of Financial Statements
- **AS 2201**: Internal Control Over Financial Reporting
- **AS 2410**: Related Parties
- **AS 2805**: Litigation, Claims, and Assessments

## Red Flag Thresholds
| Finding | Severity | Threshold |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Material weakness in IC | High | Any identified |
| Going concern flag | High | Any identified |
| Related party transactions | Medium | >5% of revenue |
| Contingent liabilities | Medium | >10% of total assets |
| Auditor adjustments | High | >3% of net income |

## Common Audit Adjustments
- Revenue recognition errors
- Expense capitalization vs expensing
- Inventory valuation errors
- Depreciation/amortization miscalculations
- Off-balance sheet item disclosures
