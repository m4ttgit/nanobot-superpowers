# Off-Balance Sheet Standards Reference

## Common Off-Balance Sheet Items
1. **Operating Leases** (pre-IFRS16/ASC842): Lease expenses not capitalized
2. **Special Purpose Entities (SPEs)**: Used for securitization, financing
3. **Variable Interest Entities (VIEs)**: Controlled but not consolidated
4. **Joint Ventures**: Equity-accounted, not consolidated
5. **Guarantees**: Corporate guarantees, performance bonds
6. **Contingent Liabilities**: Pending litigation, tax disputes
7. **Pension Obligations**: Unfunded pension deficits
8. **Forward Purchase Commitments**: Take-or-pay contracts, PPAs

## Accounting Standards
- **IFRS 16**: Leases (effective 2019) — almost all leases on-balance
- **ASC 842**: US GAAP leases (similar to IFRS 16)
- **IFRS 10**: Consolidation of special purpose entities
- **ASC 810**: Consolidation of VIEs

## Adjustment Rules
| Item | Debt-Equivalent Treatment |
|------|---------------------------|
| Operating leases | Capitalize at present value of future lease payments |
| SPE/VIE exposures | Include proportionate share of debt |
| Guarantees | Include face value if probable |
| Unfunded pension | Include deficit amount |

## Red Flag Thresholds
| Exposure | Risk Level | Threshold |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| Operating leases | Medium | >10% of EBIT |
| SPE/VIE | High | Any unconsolidated |
| Guarantees | Medium | >10% of total assets |
| Pension deficit | High | >20% of total equity |
